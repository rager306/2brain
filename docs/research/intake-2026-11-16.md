# The 76 unlinked notes were 13 — and the hole was in-degree

`tools/kb/census.py` and the engine (`tb_content`, `tb_references`, `tb_graph_health`) on the live vault, 2026-11-16,
before and after a link pass that added 34 edges inside `f/patterns` and two inbox families.

## The premise was a measurement artifact

The census counted a note as unlinked when its text had no `[[…]]` in the body and no block-list entry under `topics:`.
Two things it could not see:

- **56 of the 76 carry their topics as an inline YAML array** — `topics: ["[[x]]"]` — not as a block list (five of the
  56 are the empty `topics: []`). The regex
  `^\s*-\s*"?\[\[` matches the block form only.
- **The engine resolves both forms to the same edge.** `tb_references` on `f/patterns/pattern-budgets-as-trades.md`
  returns one outgoing `field-string-wikilink`, target `exact-discipline-deep`, from the inline array.

Measured through the engine on the same 76 paths:

| measure | count |
|---|---:|
| no link by the census regex | 76 |
| at least one outgoing reference in the engine | 63 |
| no reference in either direction | 13 |
| with nothing pointing at them (no inbound) | 48 |
| `f/patterns` notes with zero inbound | 35 of 36 |

So "76 notes with no link of any kind" was wrong in both directions: 63 already had a link (the metric missed the
form), and the measure that matters — inbound — was never computed. The census in the repo now prints this
cross-check itself; the numbers below are from that updated run.

`expected_hash` from the preceding `tb_content` read. All 34 targets resolve (`tb_graph_health` reports 0 broken links).

## What this implies

1. **A field form the census cannot parse is worse than a missing link.** It reports a hole where there is none and
   hides the one that exists. Normalize `topics:` to the block form (872 notes use it, 69 the inline array) or count
   the engine's references; the cross-check now in the census is the cheaper fix.
2. **In-degree is the reachability measure.** All 36 pattern notes had one outgoing link to their source extract and
   35 had nothing pointing at them: leaves of their source, invisible to anyone walking the graph from a hub.
   34 family-internal edges moved 16 of them to inbound ≥ 1 without a single cycle.
3. **A `family:` with one member is a dangling family id.** 28 of the 76 point at families that will never have a
   second note; the value records provenance, not a group. If families are meant to group, the census should report
   family size and treat size 1 as unset.
4. **Concurrent sessions contend on exactly the notes they share.** Two of 36 pattern notes were uncommittable
   because another session had them dirty. The clean-path precondition is correct; the operational rule it implies is
   that a lane owns its write set for the length of a pass, not that the gate should relax.
